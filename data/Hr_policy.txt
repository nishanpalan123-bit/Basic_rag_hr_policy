COMPANY HUMAN RESOURCES (HR) POLICY DOCUMENT

Company Name: ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd.
Document Type: Human Resources Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 1.0

IMPORTANT NOTE: This is a fictional sample HR policy created for educational and RAG project testing purposes. It is not an official company policy.

1. Introduction

ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is committed to maintaining a professional, inclusive, safe, and productive workplace. This HR policy defines the company's guidelines regarding employee attendance, working hours, leave, salaries, code of conduct, remote work, performance evaluations, and employee benefits.

All employees are expected to understand and follow these policies.

2. Working Hours

The standard working schedule is Monday through Friday, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Employees are entitled to a one-hour lunch break between 12:00 PM and 2:00 PM.

Employees must arrive on time and record their attendance through the company's attendance management system.

Employees who expect to arrive late must notify their reporting manager as early as possible.

Some departments may follow different schedules depending on business requirements. Any alternative schedule must be approved by the department manager and HR department.

3. Attendance Policy

All employees must record their daily attendance using the designated attendance system.

Employees who are unable to attend work because of illness or an emergency must inform their reporting manager before their scheduled starting time whenever reasonably possible.

Repeated unapproved absences or habitual lateness may result in disciplinary action.

Employees must not record attendance on behalf of another employee or manipulate attendance records.

Attendance discrepancies should be reported to HR within three working days of discovering the issue.

4. Leave Policy

Employees are eligible for the following annual leave allowances:

Earned Leave: 12 days per calendar year.
Casual Leave: 6 days per calendar year.
Sick Leave: 6 days per calendar year.
Public Holidays: As announced in the company's annual holiday calendar.

Leave allowances are prorated for employees who join during the calendar year, where applicable.

4.1 Earned Leave

Earned leave is intended for planned personal time off and vacations.

Employees should submit earned leave requests at least seven calendar days before the intended leave date.

Approval depends on team availability, workload, and business requirements.

4.2 Casual Leave

Casual leave may be used for personal matters or unexpected situations.

Employees should inform their reporting manager as soon as possible when requesting casual leave.

4.3 Sick Leave

Sick leave is available when employees are unable to work because of illness or medical reasons.

Employees should notify their reporting manager as soon as possible.

HR may request appropriate supporting documentation for extended sick leave, subject to applicable law and company procedures.

4.4 Leave Approval

All leave requests must be submitted through the company's designated leave management system.

A leave request is not considered approved until the authorized manager confirms approval.

Employees should contact HR if they have questions about leave balances or eligibility.

The leave allowances in this document are illustrative and must be verified against the employer's actual policies and applicable law before use.

5. Salary and Payroll Policy

Employee salaries are paid monthly, generally on the last working day of each month.

If the scheduled payday falls on a public holiday or non-working day, payroll will communicate the revised payment date.

Employees receive salary statements showing applicable earnings and deductions.

Salary adjustments may occur due to promotions, annual reviews, changes in responsibilities, or other approved circumstances.

Employees must notify HR promptly if their bank account details or other payroll information change.

Salary information is confidential and should be handled in accordance with company policy and applicable law.

Questions about salary payments, deductions, or discrepancies should be directed to the HR or payroll department.

6. Employee Code of Conduct

Every employee is expected to behave professionally and respectfully toward colleagues, customers, vendors, and business partners.

Employees must:

Treat colleagues fairly and respectfully.
Protect confidential company and customer information.
Follow applicable laws and company policies.
Avoid conflicts of interest or disclose potential conflicts to management.
Use company equipment and resources responsibly.
Maintain accurate business records.
Avoid harassment, discrimination, bullying, and retaliation.
Report suspected misconduct through appropriate company channels.

Violations may result in corrective or disciplinary action, depending on the nature and severity of the incident and applicable law.

7. Work From Home Policy

Eligible employees may work remotely with approval from their reporting manager.

Employees requesting remote work should submit their request through the designated company process.

Remote employees must remain available during their agreed working hours and attend required virtual meetings.

Employees must protect confidential information when working outside the office.

Company devices and approved communication tools should be used for work-related activities.

Remote work arrangements may be reviewed or modified based on business requirements, employee performance, security considerations, and applicable law.

8. Probation Policy

New employees may be placed on a probation period of three months from their date of joining.

During probation, managers evaluate job performance, attendance, communication, teamwork, and overall suitability for the role.

The probation period may be extended for up to three additional months when justified by documented performance or business considerations and permitted by applicable law.

HR communicates the outcome of the probation review to the employee.

Possible outcomes include confirmation of employment, an approved extension of probation, or another action consistent with the employment agreement and applicable law.

9. Performance Appraisal Policy

Employee performance reviews are conducted annually.

Managers assess employees based on role-specific objectives, work quality, productivity, communication, collaboration, and professional development.

Employees may be asked to complete a self-assessment before the review meeting.

Performance discussions should identify achievements, improvement areas, and development goals.

Promotions, salary increases, and bonuses are not guaranteed by a positive performance review. Decisions depend on performance, business requirements, available budgets, and applicable company procedures.

Employees may contact HR if they have questions about the appraisal process.

10. Employee Benefits

Eligible employees may receive benefits according to their employment agreements and company policies.

Benefits may include:

Health insurance.
Provident fund contributions where applicable.
Gratuity where eligibility requirements are met.
Paid public holidays.
Professional development and training opportunities.
Employee wellness initiatives.

Benefit eligibility, coverage, contribution rates, and applicable conditions are determined by the relevant benefit plan, employment terms, and applicable law.

Employees should contact HR for current benefit details and enrollment procedures.

11. Workplace Harassment and Anti-Discrimination

ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is committed to maintaining a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, bullying, and retaliation.

Discrimination or harassment based on legally protected characteristics is prohibited.

Employees who experience or witness inappropriate behavior should report the matter through the designated HR or grievance reporting channel.

Reports will be handled as promptly and confidentially as reasonably possible, consistent with a fair investigation and applicable legal requirements.

Retaliation against an employee who raises a concern in good faith or participates in an investigation is prohibited.

Where applicable, complaints involving sexual harassment must be handled through the legally required complaint mechanism and procedures.

12. Resignation and Notice Period

Employees who wish to resign must submit a written resignation to their reporting manager and HR.

The standard notice period for this sample policy is 30 calendar days, unless the employee's employment agreement or applicable law provides otherwise.

Managers and HR coordinate the employee's transition, pending responsibilities, return of company property, and exit formalities.

Notice period adjustments or waivers require appropriate authorization and must comply with applicable employment terms and law.

Final settlement is processed according to the applicable payroll schedule, employment terms, and legal requirements.

13. Exit Process

Employees leaving the company must complete the exit process.

The exit process may include:

Completing knowledge transfer.
Returning laptops, identity cards, and other company property.
Transferring work documents and responsibilities.
Completing required clearance procedures.
Participating in an exit interview, where applicable.
Verifying outstanding salary, leave balances, and other settlement items.

HR coordinates the exit process and communicates the required documentation and timelines.

14. Confidentiality and Data Protection

Employees must protect confidential business information, employee records, customer information, passwords, and intellectual property.

Confidential information must only be accessed or shared for authorized business purposes.

Employees must follow company information security procedures and applicable data protection laws.

Suspected data breaches, unauthorized access, or accidental disclosure must be reported promptly to the designated IT or security team.

Employees must not share confidential company information through unauthorized applications, personal accounts, or public platforms.

These responsibilities may continue after employment ends, subject to applicable agreements and law.

15. Training and Professional Development

The company encourages employees to develop their technical, professional, and interpersonal skills.

Employees may participate in approved training sessions, workshops, certification programs, and knowledge-sharing activities.

Managers should identify relevant learning opportunities during performance discussions.

Training budgets and reimbursements require prior approval and are subject to company guidelines.

Employees seeking training support should contact their reporting manager or HR department.

16. Grievance Redressal Policy

Employees may raise workplace concerns regarding management decisions, interpersonal conflicts, compensation, working conditions, discrimination, or other employment matters.

Employees should submit grievances to HR through the designated reporting channel.

HR will acknowledge the complaint, review the available information, and determine appropriate next steps.

Where necessary, HR may arrange meetings with the employee and other relevant individuals.

The company will make reasonable efforts to handle grievances fairly and promptly.

Employees will be protected against retaliation for raising legitimate concerns in good faith.

Matters requiring a legally prescribed complaint process must follow the applicable procedure.

17. Disciplinary Policy

Employees are expected to comply with company policies and reasonable workplace instructions.

Depending on the circumstances, violations may lead to:

Verbal counseling.
Written warning.
Formal performance improvement measures.
Suspension where lawful and appropriate.
Termination of employment where justified and permitted by applicable law.

The company will consider the seriousness of the violation, relevant evidence, prior incidents, and the employee's opportunity to respond.

Serious misconduct may require immediate investigation and appropriate protective measures.

Disciplinary action must comply with applicable law and the employee's employment terms.

18. Policy Review and Amendments

HR is responsible for reviewing this policy periodically.

The company may amend its policies to reflect operational changes, legal requirements, or updated business practices.

Employees will be informed of material changes through appropriate communication channels.

Where a policy conflicts with applicable law or a legally binding employment term, the relevant law or binding term will take precedence.

Employees should contact HR for clarification about any provision of this document.

19. Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What are the standard working hours?

Answer: The standard schedule is Monday through Friday, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, with a one-hour lunch break.

Q2. How many earned leave days are provided annually?

Answer: The sample policy provides 12 earned leave days per calendar year, subject to eligibility and applicable conditions.

Q3. How many casual leave days are provided annually?

Answer: The sample policy provides 6 casual leave days per calendar year.

Q4. How many sick leave days are provided annually?

Answer: The sample policy provides 6 sick leave days per calendar year.

Q5. When are salaries paid?

Answer: Salaries are generally paid on the last working day of each month.

Q6. What is the probation period?

Answer: The standard probation period is three months and may be extended for up to three additional months under the conditions described in the policy.

Q7. Can employees work from home?

Answer: Eligible employees may work remotely with approval from their reporting manager.

Q8. How much notice must an employee provide before resigning?

Answer: The sample policy specifies 30 calendar days unless the employment agreement or applicable law provides otherwise.

Q9. How often are performance appraisals conducted?

Answer: Performance reviews are conducted annually.

Q10. Whom should employees contact about salary discrepancies?

Answer: Employees should contact HR or the payroll department.

Q11. What should an employee do if they are sick and cannot attend work?

Answer: The employee should notify their reporting manager as soon as possible and follow the sick leave procedure.

Q12. How can an employee report workplace harassment?

Answer: The employee should use the designated HR or grievance reporting channel and any applicable legally required complaint mechanism.

Q13. Can unused leave be carried forward?

Answer: This sample policy does not specify a general leave carry-forward rule. Employees should contact HR to confirm the applicable policy.

Q14. Are bonuses guaranteed after a positive performance appraisal?

Answer: No. Bonuses depend on company procedures, business requirements, performance, and available budgets.

Q15. What should an employee do if company property is lost?

Answer: The employee should promptly report the incident to their manager and the relevant IT, security, or administrative team.

20. Contact Information

For HR-related questions, employees should contact the Human Resources Department through the company's official internal communication channel.

Employees should consult their employment agreement and the latest approved company policy for authoritative information.
